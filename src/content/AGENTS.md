# Content Instructions

These instructions apply to content under `src/content/`.

Also follow the repository-root `AGENTS.md`.

## Accuracy

Never invent:

- project roles;
- dates;
- clients;
- metrics;
- awards;
- technologies;
- testimonials;
- outcomes.

If information is not confirmed:

- leave it out; or
- keep the entry as `draft`.

## Style

Project writing should be:

- concise;
- concrete;
- professional;
- visually scannable;
- free of generic marketing filler.

## Media

Every meaningful cover/image must have an accurate alt description.

Do not describe purely decorative visual details in alt text if they add no useful information.

## Drafts

Incomplete content must not be published merely to fill the grid.
