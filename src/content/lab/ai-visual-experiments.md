## Overview

AI Visual Experiments is an ongoing space for testing how generative image tools
can support art direction without replacing visual judgment. Each study begins
with a defined use case, a composition goal and a clear set of constraints.

## Focus

The experiments cover visual research, image generation, controlled variations,
editing and the creation of coherent asset families. Particular attention is
given to framing, material, light, palette and the removal of generic visual
patterns.

## Workflow

Prompts are treated as production briefs rather than isolated descriptions.
Outputs are reviewed against the intended context, then refined through focused
iterations. Selected images are prepared for responsive web use and documented
so that the direction can be reproduced or extended later.

## Perspective

The objective is not maximum automation. It is a more deliberate relationship
between concept, tool and final selection, where the designer remains responsible
for coherence, accuracy and meaning.
